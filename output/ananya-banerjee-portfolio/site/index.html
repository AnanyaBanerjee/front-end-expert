What I believe

Technology should do more than impress us.

I love technology, but I'm less interested in technology for technology's sake.

I'm interested in what it lets people do, understand, feel and become.

That's why my work jumps between AI agents and puzzle games, productivity and mental wellbeing, storytelling and entertainment. They look unrelated from the outside. To me, they're all versions of the same question:

Can I make this experience a little better?
