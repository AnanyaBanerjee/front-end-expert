# Portfolio design

Approved direction: interactive venture gallery (user: “do it”, 2026-09-18).

## Audience and purpose
People discovering Ananya’s products, collaborators, and readers. Let visitors explore the breadth of her work and open each venture’s actual destination.

## Visual thesis
An open, bright creative studio: crisp neutral canvas, confident oversized editorial typography, a restrained red accent, and product-specific colors confined to previews. Brand voice: curious, prolific, human. Use Manrope with system sans fallback. Preserve the static HTML architecture; no runtime framework or third-party scripts are needed.

## Experience
A concise introduction leads straight into a varied two-column venture gallery. Each tile has an always-visible name and description. Hover/focus animates its preview; a labeled button expands features and verified external destinations. On touch, the same button opens details. Escape closes the active preview. No required information is available only on hover. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

Separate sections explain the connected AI ecosystem, quote-to-publishing workflow, writing and media ventures, and earlier experiments. No invented counts, audiences, testimonials, or release claims. Include prior ventures named on the old site, with conservative historical labels when current status is unverified.

## Content and privacy
Use local product screenshots and factual capability diagrams. Never invent social profiles. Social previews are self-hosted visuals with outbound links, not tracking embeds. No analytics, forms, cookies, sign-in, or backend. Missing media or metrics should not become fake proof. Legal pages share all visual tokens and navigation.

## Delivery
Build and validate a local reviewable site first. Existing live domain remains unchanged. Public publishing requires a separate final action after review, consistent with the user’s standing rules.
