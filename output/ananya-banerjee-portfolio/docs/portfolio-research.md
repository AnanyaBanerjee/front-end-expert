## Ranked interaction directions for selection

1. **Interactive venture gallery:** distinct branded tiles; hover or keyboard focus reveals a short product/media preview, capabilities, and links. Tap opens the equivalent panel on mobile. Recommended for broad coverage and clarity.
2. **Ecosystem map:** connected clusters expose relationships; selecting a venture opens details. Stronger relationship storytelling but harder to scan with many ventures.
3. **Editorial showcase:** large project sections with restrained previews. Strong storytelling, with more scrolling to cover the full inventory.

These are proposals, not approved animation specifications. Core descriptions and navigation remain accessible without hover; reduced-motion behavior must be designed alongside motion.
