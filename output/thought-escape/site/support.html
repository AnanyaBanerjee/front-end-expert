Common issues

How do I restore my in-app purchases on a new device?
  1. Open Thought Escape on the new device.
  2. Sign in to the App Store or Google Play with the same account that made the original purchase.
  3. Go to Settings → In-App Purchases → Restore Purchases.
  4. Non-consumable items (Board Skins, Lifetime Remove Ads) return within seconds. Active Remove Ads subscriptions restore automatically when you sign in with the same account.

Consumable items (Escape Shards, World Currency, Extra Moves, Restore Lives) do not restore by design — Apple and Google don't support consumable restores. If your shards didn't credit the first time, email the receipt and they will be granted manually.

My streak reset even though I played yesterday.

Streaks are local-time based and roll over at midnight in your device's timezone. The most common causes:

  • Travel across timezones. If you played late at night in one timezone and then your phone moved to a timezone where the day already rolled over, the boundary can skip.
  • Manual clock changes. Resetting your device's date or time can break the streak window.
  • App not opened on a given day. Streaks reset if a full local day passes without you opening the app.

If none of these explain it, email a screenshot of the streak screen plus your timezone and Ananya will look into it.

My lives don't seem to be resetting.

Lives refresh at midnight local time. The pools (campaign, daily, special challenge) are separate and each one resets independently.

If your campaign lives are stuck at zero after midnight:

  1. Force-quit Thought Escape and reopen it. The reset check fires on launch.
  2. Check your device's date and time settings — they should be set to automatic.
  3. If still stuck, email a screenshot of the lives counter.
The game is stuck or feels frozen between levels.

Try this in order:

  1. Tap anywhere on the screen first — sometimes the victory animation is still playing.
  2. Force-quit and reopen the app.
  3. Restart the phone.
  4. If you're on an older device (iPhone X or earlier), make sure no other heavy apps are running in the background.

If the freeze keeps happening on the same level, email the level number — every freeze report helps fix the issue.

I can't hear sound or feel haptics.
  • No sound: Check that your device isn't in silent mode (orange switch on iPhone). Then go to Settings → Sound in Thought Escape and confirm it's on. Sounds also lower on top of the system volume slider.
  • No haptics: On iPhone, check Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics. On iPad, haptics are not supported by hardware — the toggle is intentionally disabled there.
How do I delete my account or progress?

To delete local progress (campaign, streak, currencies, inventory): open Settings → Reset Progress. This is permanent.

To delete your cloud profile (username and email used for the leaderboard): email Ananya from the email tied to the account. The cloud profile is removed within 7 days.

See the privacy policy for the full data-deletion rights.

Purchases & Subscriptions

All purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. To cancel a subscription, open your App Store or Google Play account settings, find Thought Escape, and tap Cancel. You keep access through the end of the current billing period — no further charges are made after that.

When emailing about a bug, please include: device model (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro), iOS version (e.g. iOS 18.4), and a one-sentence description of what you tapped and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
