Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026

This is the privacy policy for Thought Escape, a mobile puzzle game built and operated by Ananya Banerjee (the "Developer"). This policy describes what the Developer collects, how it is used, and how it is kept safe.

What Thought Escape collects

Thought Escape is designed to be private by default. No account is required to play. Most of your game state — progress, streak, currencies, settings — lives entirely on your device.

  • Game progress. Your campaign progress, streak, tool inventory, board skin selections, and settings are stored on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to enable cloud sync.
  • Account information (optional). If you create a profile in Settings, your chosen username and email address are stored securely in the cloud so your scores appear on the leaderboard and sync across your devices. Email is optional. You can delete your profile at any time.
  • Usage data. Anonymous information about how you play — such as which levels you completed, which tools you used, and whether you finished the daily puzzle — is collected to help improve the game. This data is aggregated and cannot identify you unless you have created a profile.
  • Diagnostic data. If the app crashes, technical information including your device model and operating system version is collected automatically to help fix the issue. No personal information is included in crash reports.
  • Payment information. All purchases are processed directly by Apple App Store or Google Play. The Developer never receives or stores your payment details.
  • Advertising identifier (optional). If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, an advertising identifier may be used to serve a relevant ad. On iOS, you control this through the App Tracking Transparency prompt shown on first launch. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

What Thought Escape does not collect

  • Your real name, postal address, or phone number.
  • Access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or calendar — the app never requests any of these.
  • Any information from outside the game itself.

How your data is used

Data collected by Thought Escape is used only to operate and improve the game — including syncing your progress, fixing crashes, processing purchases, and serving rewarded ads you choose to watch. Your data is never sold. It is never used to build advertising profiles or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Service providers

The Developer works with a small number of trusted third-party service providers to operate Thought Escape — covering areas such as cloud storage, anonymous analytics, crash reporting, purchase processing, and optional advertising. These providers are authorised to use your data only to perform the specific service they were engaged for, and are contractually prohibited from using it for any other purpose. All payment processing is handled directly by Apple App Store or Google Play.

Children

Thought Escape is rated for all ages and is suitable for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information, contact the Developer at the address below and it will be removed.

Your rights

You can:

  • Delete your local data at any time by deleting the app or using "Reset progress" in Settings.
  • Delete your cloud profile by contacting the Developer at the address below. Your username and email will be removed from the leaderboard within 7 days.
  • Disable advertising tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking, or by tapping "Ask App not to Track" when prompted.
  • Disable analytics by toggling Analytics off in Thought Escape's Settings screen (coming in a future update).

If you are based in the EU or UK, you have additional rights under GDPR — including the right to a copy of your data, the right to correction, and the right to object to processing. Contact the Developer to exercise any of these rights.

If you are based in California, the CCPA gives you the right to know what is collected, the right to delete, and the right to opt out of sale of personal information. Thought Escape does not sell personal information.

Data retention

Local data is kept until you delete the app or reset progress. Cloud profile data is kept until you request deletion. Anonymous analytics events are kept for up to 24 months.

Security

Security is built into Thought Escape from the ground up, not added as an afterthought. All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Cloud-stored profiles are isolated at the data layer — your records can only be read and modified by you. The Developer reviews and updates security practices on an ongoing basis.

You remain in control at every step: your core game data lives on your device, creating an account is entirely optional, and you can delete your data — locally or from the cloud — whenever you choose.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Thought Escape evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app and on this page. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or anything else, contact Ananya Banerjee at ananyabilbanerjee@gmail.com.
