The arrow rule

  1. Tap a tile. Its arrow shows where it travels.
  2. Clear path → escape. The tile slides off the board along its arrow.
  3. Blocked path → rejected. Another tile in the way? The board shakes.
  4. Chain it out. Each escape opens the next path.

Lives, tool counts, and hint icons are visual only here — the full game wires them all up.

How it plays

Tap an arrow. Watch it leave.

Each tile points one of eight ways. Tap it and it travels along its arrow until something stops it — another tile, a wall, or the board edge. Reach the edge, escape. Hit a blocker, the tap is rejected. Find the order.

  • Arrow tiles, eight directions
    Every tile faces one of eight compass directions. Plan the order and the board clears itself in one quiet cascade.
  • Sixteen mechanics, taught one at a time
    Frozen tiles, gravity, mirrors, portals, countdown, splitters, fire cells, blind tiles. Every new rule arrives with a guided concept tour before you play it for real.
  • Five difficulty tiers, adaptive
    Easy, medium, hard, expert, nightmare. The infinite pool generates levels at the tier you've earned. After any hard level the next two are gentler — by design.
  • Eleven power-up tools, owned forever
    Undo, rotate, magnet, drill, dynamite, ghost, swap, mirror drop, portal drop, freeze, hint. Unlock once, keep forever. Daily free uses replenish at midnight.
Think deeper

How to read an escape board.

The rules are simple. The strategy takes a little longer — here's how experienced players think before they tap.

01
Find the free tiles first

Look for tiles whose arrows point directly to an open board edge with nothing in the way. These escape without any other tile needing to move first. Tap those first — they're free moves that open everything else.

02
Read what's blocking, not what's free

For every tile that can't escape yet, identify exactly which tile is sitting in its path. That blocker becomes your next priority. Trace each dependency before you tap — know the chain, don't guess it.

03
Think in reverse

Start from the last tile that needs to leave and work backwards. Which tile has to escape before it? And before that one? Mentally clearing the board in reverse reveals the correct forward tap order almost automatically.

04
Save tools for genuine dead ends

Undo is powerful, but burning it on your first misstep is wasteful — what looks like a mistake sometimes opens an unexpected path two moves later. Use Rotate and Magnet when the board is truly stuck, not after a single bad tap.

05
Shaped boards have hidden shortcuts

Not every cell exists on a shaped board. Tiles pointing toward a corner that doesn't exist travel past the missing cells straight to the edge — they escape faster than they look. Diagonals on irregular shapes are often the fastest exits.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Pricing, platforms, how it's different — the things people ask before downloading.

What is Thought Escape?
Thought Escape is a meditative mobile puzzle game. You tap tiles on a shaped board to escape them off the edges, working through dependencies and escape chains across hand-tuned levels in four themed worlds: Ocean, Mountain, Forest, and Snow.
What platforms is Thought Escape on?
Thought Escape is available on iOS and coming soon to Android. Join the waitlist above to get the App Store link the moment it goes live.
How many levels does it have?
Thought Escape has hand-tuned campaign levels plus an infinite procedurally generated pool that adapts to five difficulty tiers: easy, medium, hard, expert, and nightmare. Plus a fresh daily puzzle every day.
Is Thought Escape free?
Yes — free to download, free to play all campaign levels. Optional in-app purchases are: a one-time Starter Bundle ($0.99, shown on first launch), shard packs ($1.99–$14.99), world currency packs ($0.99–$6.99), Remove Ads as a subscription ($4.99/month, $9.99/quarter, $29.99/year) or a lifetime one-time purchase ($49.99), and cosmetic board skins ($0.99–$1.99 each). Nothing gates progression. No paywall.
How is this different from Arrowscapes or Arrows Puzzle Escape?
Three differences. First, no timers on standard levels — the board waits for you. Second, no gacha mechanics — every tool unlocks by playing, and once you have it you have it forever. Third, cosmetic spend only: Remove Ads starts at $4.99/month or $49.99 lifetime — nothing behind a paywall affects gameplay. The puzzle logic is the same arrow-cascade mechanic the genre is built on, just rendered as a luxury gemstone artifact instead of a flat UI.
What are the four worlds?
Ocean (moon pearls), Mountain (sun runes), Forest (spirit seeds), and Snow (aurora shards). Each world has its own art, tile materials, sounds, and currency. Levels unlock progressively along a single linear journey.
Does it work offline?
Yes — all campaign levels and daily puzzles work fully offline. Your progress saves locally on your device. The leaderboard and cloud profile sync require a connection, but those are optional features. You can play Thought Escape on a plane with no issue.
