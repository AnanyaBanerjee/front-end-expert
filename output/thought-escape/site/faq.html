Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Thought Escape — how to play, what it costs, how it compares to other puzzle games, and how it works offline.

What is Thought Escape?
Thought Escape is a meditative mobile puzzle game. You tap tiles on a shaped board to escape them off the edges, working through dependencies and escape chains across hand-tuned levels in four themed worlds: Ocean, Mountain, Forest, and Snow.
What platforms is Thought Escape on?
Thought Escape is available now on iOS. Android is coming soon — join the waitlist at thought-escape.com to get the link the day it launches.
How many levels does it have?
Thought Escape has hand-tuned campaign levels plus a procedurally generated pool that adapts to five difficulty tiers: easy, medium, hard, expert, and nightmare. Plus a fresh daily puzzle every morning.
Is Thought Escape free?
Yes — free to download, free to play all campaign levels. Optional in-app purchases include shard packs ($1.99–$14.99), world currency packs ($0.99–$6.99), Remove Ads as a subscription ($4.99/month, $9.99/quarter, $29.99/year) or a lifetime one-time purchase ($49.99), and cosmetic board skins ($0.99–$1.99 each). Nothing gates progression. No paywall on gameplay.
How is this different from other arrow puzzle games?
Three differences. First, no timers on standard levels — the board waits for you. Second, no gacha mechanics — every tool unlocks by playing, and once you have it you keep it forever. Third, cosmetic spend only: nothing behind a paywall affects whether you can solve a level.
What are the four worlds?
Ocean (moon pearls), Mountain (sun runes), Forest (spirit seeds), and Snow (aurora shards). Each world has its own art, tile materials, sounds, and currency. Levels unlock progressively along a single linear journey.
Does it work offline?
Yes — all campaign levels and daily puzzles work fully offline. Your progress saves locally on your device. The leaderboard and cloud profile sync require a connection, but those are optional features.
Who builds Thought Escape?
Thought Escape is built by Ananya Banerjee — a solo builder shipping calm, useful apps. You can follow the build process on Ananya's Build Room on Substack.
What in-app tools are available?
Thought Escape has 11 tools: Undo, Rotate, Magnet, Extra Moves, Restore Lives, and more. All 11 unlock through gameplay — you never need to buy them to access a level.
