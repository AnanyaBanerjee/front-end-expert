How it works

A ritual, not a race.

01
A new board appears at midnight, paired with one of the four realms. Forest, Ocean, Mountain, or Snow — the world shifts with the day.
02
Every Monday the seven-shape lineup regenerates from scratch. No two weeks share the same sequence of boards.
03
Sizes range from 6×6 to 10×10 — effectively infinite distinct boards. The shape changes, the rules stay the same.
04
Same rules as the main game — only the canvas changes. Arrow tiles, eight directions, chain escapes. Just a new shape every morning.
